## 5. Content governance

Every service page should have an owner/reviewer.

Technical claims should be checked by the business/technical lead.

Every case study must be based on: - real project - real scope - real
images - real location - permission to publish client information

Every testimonial must be genuine and approved.

## 6. Google policy reminder

Do not create: - fake reviews - incentivized reviews - fake project
evidence - mass low-value AI pages - keyword-stuffed content

Use AI as an editing/research assistant, but final public content should
be useful, original and factually reviewed.
